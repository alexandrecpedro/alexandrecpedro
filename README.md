## 🚀 About Me

I am a **Data Engineer & Analytics Engineer**, combining an analytical foundation in **Chemical Engineering** with robust software engineering with AI and cloud data architecture expertise.

My recent professional milestones include:
- **Critical Pipeline Recovery:** Led incident response for e-commerce payment integrations during peak sales, protecting an estimated **R\$4M in conversions**.
- **Cost Optimization & BI Transition:** Evaluated and spearheaded a migration to **Looker**, successfully avoiding a 2x licensing price spike and achieving a **~50% reduction in monthly analytics costs**.
- **Data Governance & Compliance:** Engineered automated hash-based rules for PII fields and data masking to align with strict data protection regulations (LGPD).
